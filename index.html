gloamgate

The maze is cut into sealed sections. Colored gates connect them in pairs: step into a rim and you come out at its twin. Find the brass key, then take the stairs down.

Your flashlight drains its battery, and the spares hidden in dead ends only add a fifth of a charge each. Something else is down here. It is faster than you walk but slower than you sprint. It sees your light from far off and hears you move nearby. With the light off and your feet still, it cannot find you.

W A S D or arrows to walk, hold Shift to sprint. The mouse aims the light. F or Space turns it on and off.
On a phone, drag anywhere to walk, hold Run to sprint, and tap the light button.
